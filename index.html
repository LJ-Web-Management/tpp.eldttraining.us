The ELDT Partner Program gives any business, including associations, staffing agencies, contractors, distributors, or consultants, a dedicated landing page where their audience can purchase ELDT theory training, listed on the FMCSA Training Provider Registry, directly from ELDTTraining.us.

A Dedicated Landing Page

Revenue & Ownership

Simple, Transparent Revenue Structure

ELDTTraining.us is operated by HAZWOPER OSHA Training, the accredited provider and seller of record for every course sold through a partner landing page. Course revenue is split 50/50 between ELDTTraining.us and the partner.

Partners can direct their own customers to purchase training themselves, or place orders on their customers' behalf. Either way, checkout, certificates, and compliance recordkeeping are handled entirely by our team.
