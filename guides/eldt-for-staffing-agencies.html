ELDT for Staffing Agencies: Build a CDL Driver Pipeline and New Revenue

Staffing agencies and workforce programs are often the first stop for people who want to become CDL drivers. ELDT theory is the natural first training step for those candidates, and offering it can turn your candidate pool into a qualified driver pipeline.

Updated October 25, 2026 3 min read By the HAZWOPER OSHA Training team, an FMCSA TPR-listed ELDT theory provider

Key Takeaways

  • Candidates without a CDL need ELDT before they can test, so theory is the first training step you can offer them.
  • Offering ELDT theory through a branded partner page keeps candidates engaged with your agency instead of drifting to a school or competitor.
  • In a partner model you earn 50% of every course sold while the registered provider handles compliance, reporting, and support.

The driver pipeline problem

Driver staffing agencies have plenty of people who want to drive and plenty of clients who need drivers. The gap is qualification: a candidate without a CDL can't be placed in a CDL role, and getting that CDL now requires ELDT.

If your agency simply tells candidates to "go get a CDL," many never return. If you guide them through the first training step yourself, they stay connected to you as they progress.

ELDT theory as the first step

A typical path for a new candidate looks like this:

  1. Commercial learner's permit (CLP). The candidate passes the state knowledge tests.
  2. ELDT theory. Completed online at the candidate's own pace, often in a week or two around their current job.
  3. Behind-the-wheel training with a CDL school or a client carrier's training program.
  4. Skills test and CDL issuance.
  5. Placement with your client.

Steps 2 and 5 are where your agency can add the most value. By offering theory directly, you keep the candidate in your ecosystem from day one.

For workforce boards and reentry programs

Workforce development boards, community organizations, and reentry programs often fund CDL training as a path to stable, well-paying work. Online theory with a bilingual toggle is a cost-effective way to start participants on that path, and lets case managers track progress before committing funds to behind-the-wheel training. Confirm funding eligibility rules with your program administrator.

Ideas for promoting your training page

  • Add the link to job postings that require a CDL: "Don't have your CDL yet? Start here."
  • Include it in candidate onboarding emails and texts.
  • Share it at job fairs and community events.
  • Offer to cover the cost of theory for candidates who commit to a placement.
  • Promote the Spanish option to reach bilingual candidates.

Frequently asked questions

Can a staffing agency offer ELDT training without being a registered provider?
Yes. Through the ELDT Partner Program, HAZWOPER OSHA Training remains the registered provider and seller of record, while the agency offers training through its own dedicated landing page.
How much does a staffing agency earn from ELDT courses?
Partners earn 50% of the revenue from every course sold through their ELDT Partner Program landing page.
Can our agency purchase training for candidates?
Yes. Partners can let candidates buy courses directly or purchase training on their behalf.
