Adding ELDT Training to Your Business: A Revenue Guide for Associations, Consultants, and Industry Suppliers

If drivers, carriers, or fleet managers already trust you, you're in a strong position to offer them ELDT training. Here's how associations, consultants, and suppliers are adding a training revenue stream without becoming training companies.

Updated October 25, 2026 3 min read By the ELDTTraining.us team, an FMCSA TPR-listed ELDT theory provider

Key Takeaways

  • ELDT theory is a recurring need: every new CDL, upgrade, and first-time P, S, or H endorsement requires it.
  • Organizations with an engaged audience can offer it through your own partner landing page with no setup cost.
  • The registered provider handles delivery, compliance, and support; you earn 50% of every sale.

Who this is for

This model fits any organization that regularly talks to CDL drivers or the companies that employ them, such as:

  • Trucking and transportation associations looking for member benefits and non-dues revenue
  • Safety and compliance consultants who advise carriers on DOT requirements
  • Insurance agencies that work with commercial auto and fleet clients
  • Truck, bus, and equipment dealers whose customers hire drivers
  • Driver qualification and drug testing providers
  • Industry publications and online communities for drivers
  • Community colleges and adult education programs without a full CDL program

Why ELDT is a good fit

  • It's required. You're not convincing anyone they need it; you're making it easy to get.
  • It recurs. Every new hire, every upgrade, and every new endorsement creates another purchase.
  • It's relevant. Offering compliant, registered training reinforces your role as a trusted resource.
  • It's online. Nothing to schedule, ship, or staff.

How the partner model works

  1. Apply. Tell us about your organization and audience.
  2. Launch. We build a dedicated landing page with the full ELDT theory library. There's no setup fee.
  3. Share. Promote the page to your members, clients, or customers.
  4. Earn. ELDTTraining.us handles checkout, certificates, TPR reporting, and support, and splits every sale 50/50 with you.

See full details on the ELDT Partner Program page, including program terms.

A simple promotion playbook

For associations

Add ELDT training to your member benefits page, mention it in your newsletter, and feature it at conferences. Consider buying training on behalf of members' new hires as a sponsorship.

For consultants

Include the link in compliance audits and onboarding checklists. When a client hires a new driver or adds hazmat lanes, point them to your page.

For suppliers and dealers

Share the page with fleet customers when they add vehicles. New trucks often mean new drivers or upgrades.

For publications and communities

Publish helpful content about ELDT (like the guides on this site) and link to your training page as the next step.

Frequently asked questions

Who can join the ELDT Partner Program?
Any business or organization with an audience that needs ELDT training, including associations, consultants, staffing agencies, schools, fleets, suppliers, school districts, and government agencies.
Is there a cost to become a partner?
