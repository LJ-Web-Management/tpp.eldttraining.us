How CDL Schools Can Outsource ELDT Theory and Focus on Behind-the-Wheel

Your trucks, ranges, and instructors are your competitive advantage. Classroom theory isn't. Here's why more CDL schools are moving ELDT theory online with a registered partner, and how to do it without losing revenue.

Updated October 25, 2026 3 min read By the HAZWOPER OSHA Training team, an FMCSA TPR-listed ELDT theory provider

Key Takeaways

  • Theory and behind-the-wheel can be delivered by different registered providers, so a school can keep BTW and outsource theory.
  • Online theory frees instructors for in-truck time, lets students start immediately, and removes curriculum maintenance from your plate.
  • A partner page lets you keep offering theory under your relationship with the student while earning 50% of every theory sale.

The classroom squeeze

For most CDL schools, the constraint is behind-the-wheel capacity: trucks, range time, and qualified instructors. Every hour an instructor spends teaching theory in a classroom is an hour they're not in a truck generating BTW completions.

Meanwhile, maintaining an ELDT-compliant theory curriculum for Class A, Class B, the B-to-A upgrade, Passenger, School Bus, and Hazmat is a full-time job of its own, and one that doesn't differentiate your school.

The split model

Under the ELDT rule, a student can complete theory with one registered provider and behind-the-wheel with another. Each reports its own portion to the Training Provider Registry. That makes a clean division of labor possible:

  • Your school stays registered for behind-the-wheel and does what it does best.
  • A theory partner delivers online theory, assessments, certificates, and TPR reporting for theory.

Students arrive at their first range day having already passed theory, so instructors can get straight to driving.

Benefits for your school

  • More BTW throughput from the same instructors and trucks.
  • Faster starts. Students begin theory the day they enroll instead of waiting for the next classroom cohort.
  • No curriculum maintenance or theory assessment management.
  • Spanish-language access with an English/Spanish toggle on every course.
  • Endorsement add-ons like Hazmat, Passenger, and School Bus without building new courses.
  • Continuing revenue on theory through a revenue share.

Keeping theory revenue

The biggest worry schools have about outsourcing theory is losing that revenue. A partner model solves it. With the ELDT Partner Program, your school gets a dedicated landing page offering the full theory library. Students buy through your page (or you buy on their behalf as part of tuition), HAZWOPER OSHA Training handles delivery and compliance, and every sale is split 50/50 with you.

You can also send graduates back to your page later when they need an upgrade or a new endorsement, which turns one-time students into long-term customers.

Put your instructors back in the truck. Add online ELDT theory to your school at no cost.

Frequently asked questions

Can a CDL school outsource ELDT theory training?
Yes. FMCSA allows theory and behind-the-wheel training to be completed with different registered providers, so a school can deliver behind-the-wheel training and use a partner for theory.
Do students need to finish theory before behind-the-wheel?
Federal rules don't mandate an order, but both must be completed and reported before the skills test. Many schools have students complete theory first.
Can my school include theory in its tuition package?
Yes. Partners can purchase courses on their students' behalf through their landing page, so theory can be bundled into tuition.

This guide is for general information and reflects FMCSA ELDT requirements as of October 25, 2026. Regulations and state policies change; confirm current requirements with FMCSA and your state driver licensing agency before making training or compliance decisions.
